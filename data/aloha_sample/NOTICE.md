This is public simulated bimanual insertion data, not real robot collection. Five complete episodes check the 14-channel, 50 FPS contract. Metadata describes all 50 source episodes. The manifest selects the five analysis episodes; original metadata/shards remain unchanged. No video is fetched. Action units/control mode are unknown until separately substantiated.
